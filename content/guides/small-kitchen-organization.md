## Start with what you use every day

Keep the things you reach for daily within easy reach, and move the rest higher up or further back.

- Group similar items together
- Use the counter only for what you use daily
- Store rarely used items out of the way

## Make use of vertical space

Shelves, racks and hooks can give a small kitchen more room without taking more floor space.

1. Look at the empty wall space
2. Measure before you choose anything
3. Keep heavy items low
