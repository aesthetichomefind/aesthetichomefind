Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

This page explains what happens to information when you visit {{brandName}}.

What we collect

The site has no accounts, comments, sign-up forms or checkout. You do not need to give us any personal information to browse.

The site does not currently use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies, and it does not set cookies of its own. Searching and filtering finds happens in your browser; what you type is not sent to us.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may record technical details such as your IP address and browser type when a page is requested. That is handled by GitHub under its own privacy statement.

Links to other sites

Product links go to Amazon, and we link to our Instagram page. When you follow one of these links, that site may set cookies and collect information under its own privacy policy. For Amazon affiliate links, Amazon uses this to know that a visit came from our site, which is how we can earn a commission. We do not control these sites.

If you contact us

If you send us an email, we use your email address and message only to reply to you. We do not sell your details.

Children

This site is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If we add analytics or advertising (such as Google AdSense) in the future, we will update this page before they are switched on.

Questions

Please use the contact page. See also our affiliate disclosure and terms.
